# Shift Shock

> Approval note: approved provisionally by the learner's relay assistant under the learner's instruction to proceed; product answers marked [AI-provisional] await the learner's confirmation.

A same-day shift fixer: when a helper at a small volunteer event calls in absent, it finds the smallest reshuffle that keeps every post covered and explains every move. [AI-provisional]

## The Unique Kernel
[AI-provisional] The **smallest** reshuffle, with a plain-words reason for every move, and an honest "cannot be covered" that names the post and the reason. Not just any valid roster: the one that moves the fewest people.

## Who It's For
[AI-provisional] The volunteer coordinator of a small event (school festival, community meetup, charity booth) with about 6 to 10 helpers. Today they reshuffle people in their head under time pressure, and the obvious swap often empties another post.

## The Core Loop
[AI-provisional] Open the preset, mark a helper absent, press "Find the smallest fix", see numbered moves with reasons, then download or copy the new roster.

## Inspiration & Identity
[AI-provisional] A printed duty board: cream card stock, pegboard columns, paper-tag helper cards. Signal-red for empty posts and absences, forest-green for covered posts. No named external references were given.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
Not established. The learner chose this idea from nine candidates generated by another AI (OpenAI Codex) from the public hackathon brief only. [Learner fact] No personal motivation was stated.

## What "Working" Looks Like
[AI-provisional] Preset "School festival" (7 helpers):
- Haru (Reception) absent → 2 moves: Ken Guide → Reception, Mina floating → Guide. Each move has a reason.
- Aiko (the only First aid) absent → "First aid cannot be covered", with the reason and the best partial plan. This is the "oh, that's cool" beat: it refuses to pretend.

## The POC Boundary
[AI-provisional]
- One time slot, up to 10 helpers.
- Posts: Reception, Guide, Stage, Kitchen, First aid; each needs 1 person.
- Skills as checkboxes per helper.
- Static web app that runs in the browser only (technical detail is in `spec.md`).

## Later
[AI-provisional] Multi-slot schedules; fairness or preference scoring; more than one person per post.

## Explicitly Cut
[AI-provisional]
- Multi-slot schedules — one slot proves the kernel.
- Messaging helpers — outside the browser-only boundary.
- Accounts and saving to a server — nothing leaves the browser; layouts can be exported instead.
- Preferences or fairness scoring — it would blur the "smallest change" kernel.
- Real staff data — the preset uses made-up names.
